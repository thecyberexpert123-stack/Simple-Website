The Cosmic Calendar

The Big Bang

Space, time, matter and energy come into being.

13.8 billion years ago

On this scale, all of recorded human history — every empire, every war, every book, every song — fits into the last 12 seconds before midnight. Everything since the Moon landing occupies about a tenth of a second.

    We are the universe
    looking back at itself.
